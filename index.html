🚀 Video Publishing & Engagement

Direct Video Publishing & Audience Engagement Suite

Edgaged Studio helps educational creators seamlessly upload, schedule, and publish video shorts directly to TikTok, driving student engagement through optimized educational metadata and targeted hashtags.

Publishing & Engagement Features

Engineered for creator efficiency, upload reliability, and community reach

📤 TikTok Direct Video Uploads

Direct chunked video file transfer to TikTok servers via the official TikTok Content Posting API with instant publish status tracking.

🎯 Engagement & Tag Optimization

Automates video captions, educational descriptions, and trending learning tags (#LearnOnTikTok, #STEM, #Science) to maximize discoverability.

⏱️ Scheduled Video Delivery

Queues video uploads at optimal peak-learning hours across the week, ensuring regular content flow and higher viewer retention.

🔒 Creator OAuth & Security

Seamless one-click account authorization through TikTok Login Kit with zero third-party token sharing and full permission revocation control.

How Publishing Works

A reliable end-to-end publishing pipeline connecting creators to their audiences

1. Secure Channel Link

Authorizes the creator account via TikTok OAuth 2.0 to establish verified publishing permissions.

2. Content & Tag Preparation

Sets educational video title, topic summary, interactive prompts, and relevant subject tags.

3. Direct API Upload

Initiates direct video upload session and securely transmits the high-definition video file.

4. Live Post & Verification

Monitors TikTok publish status to ensure successful delivery to the public feed for viewers.

Empowering Educational Content Creators

Edgaged Studio streamlines educational video delivery, allowing educators to focus on teaching while the platform handles automated publishing, scheduling, and channel engagement.
