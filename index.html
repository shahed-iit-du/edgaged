🚀 Automated Video Pipeline

Intelligent STEM Educational Video Generator & Publisher

Edgaged Studio is an automated pipeline that ingests curriculum concepts, drafts intuitive storyboards, generates programmatic Manim animations, and seamlessly publishes bite-sized educational video shorts to verified TikTok and social channels.

Core Platform Features

Purpose-built for science, math, and STEM educational content creation

🎬 Storyboard & Manim Engine

Transforms complex scientific formulas, physics concepts, and algebraic models into high-definition, 60fps programmatic animations using the Manim mathematical animation framework.

🎙️ Synchronized AI Voiceover

Generates high-clarity educational narrations and perfectly aligns subtitle timings with on-screen mathematical scene transitions.

📱 TikTok Direct Video Publishing

Utilizes the official TikTok Content Posting API to directly publish finished educational video shorts with optimized descriptions, tags, and educational metadata.

🔍 Automated Visual QA

Runs headless frame analysis and visual inspection on every scene layout to prevent overlapping text, coordinate mismatches, or aspect ratio defects.

Publishing Pipeline Workflow

From textbook concept to published social video in four automated stages

1. Curriculum Ingestion

Extracts lesson objectives and mathematical theorems from structured learning playlists.

2. Animation Scripting

Generates precise vector math animations, coordinate grids, and visual demonstrations.

3. Audio & Stitching

Binds audio narration, background music, and subtitles into portrait 9:16 video shorts.

4. TikTok Direct Post

Securely authorizes channel ownership via OAuth 2.0 and uploads the short to TikTok.

Built for Educational Creators

Edgaged is an internal creator tool designed to bring engaging, accurate, and visually stunning math and physics lessons to TikTok learners worldwide.
