Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Edgaged Studio ("we," "our," or "the Service") collects, uses, and protects your information when using our automated video generation and publishing platform.

1. Information We Collect and Access

When you authenticate your TikTok account via OAuth 2.0 Login Kit, we access only the minimal scopes necessary:

  • Basic Profile Info (user.info.basic): Used solely to identify your connected channel ID (open_id) and display name.
  • Video Upload & Publishing Permissions (video.upload, video.publish): Used strictly to upload generated video files and post educational video shorts to your account upon pipeline completion.

2. How Data is Handled and Stored

We believe in strict data privacy and local ownership:

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored strictly within the private local configuration on your self-hosted instance.
  • We do not operate external third-party data tracking or centralized analytics servers that harvest user information.
  • We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade any personal data or TikTok account details.

3. Third-Party API Services

Edgaged Studio interfaces directly with the official TikTok Content Posting API. Your use of TikTok integration is subject to the TikTok Terms of Service and TikTok Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You can revoke access to Edgaged Studio at any time directly through your TikTok account settings (under Security $\rightarrow$ Manage App Permissions). Deleting your local configuration token permanently severs all application connectivity.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact edgaged46@gmail.com.
