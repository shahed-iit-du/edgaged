Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how Edgaged Studio ("we," "our," or "the Service") handles account data and video publishing permissions when interacting with TikTok.

1. Information We Access via Official APIs

To enable direct publishing and channel engagement tracking, our application requests minimal necessary OAuth scopes:

  • Basic Profile Identity (user.info.basic): Used exclusively to identify the authorized creator account and channel display name.
  • Content Posting Permissions (video.upload, video.publish): Required to initiate chunked video file uploads, transfer educational MP4 shorts to TikTok servers, and publish videos with relevant tags and descriptions.

2. Data Handling, Storage & Security

We enforce strict creator privacy and localized data storage:

  • OAuth credentials and access tokens are stored securely in local configuration files on the creator's host machine.
  • We do not operate tracking databases, centralized data collection pipelines, or analytics scrapers.
  • We never sell, rent, share, or monetize creator account details or private information.

3. TikTok Platform Compliance

Edgaged Studio interacts with TikTok strictly through the official TikTok Content Posting API. Use of our service is governed by the TikTok Terms of Service and TikTok Privacy Policy.

4. User Rights & Permission Revocation

Creators maintain total control over application permissions. You may revoke Edgaged Studio's access at any time directly through TikTok (Settings and privacy $\rightarrow$ Security $\rightarrow$ Manage app permissions).

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact edgaged46@gmail.com.
